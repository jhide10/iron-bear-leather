The Making
01

Mark the pattern

Full grain leather picked off the hide, the pattern traced on by hand before a single cut is made. Every piece starts here.

02

Cut by hand

Cut to the line with a knife and a steel rule. Every panel is cut one at a time — no press, no die.

03

Stitch it by hand

Saddle stitched two needles at a time — the seam that does not unravel when one thread gives. No machine shortcuts, no skipped passes.

04

Finished piece

Edges bevelled, sanded and burnished, hardware set, then sealed. It leaves the bench only when it is right.
